# The Inkadra name and logo

Inkadra's code is free software: you can use, study, change and share it under the licences in
the [README](README.md#licence). The **name "Inkadra" and the Inkadra logo are not part of those
licences**. They identify this project and the services run by its maintainer, so that people
know what they are getting. Neither the AGPL nor the MPL gives a right to use them.

This page says what you can do with the name and logo without asking, and what needs permission.
It is meant to be fair, not to get in the way of using the software.

## You can, without asking

- Say truthfully that something **is, uses or works with Inkadra**: "we host Inkadra for film
  crews", "a plugin for Inkadra", "based on Inkadra", "compatible with Inkadra".
- Run Inkadra unmodified for yourself, your crew or your company and call it Inkadra.
- Write about Inkadra, teach it, review it, and show screenshots of it.
- Use the logo to link to this project (for example in a list of the tools you use), unchanged.

## Please do not, unless you have written permission

- Call a **modified version** or a fork "Inkadra", or give it a name or logo that could be
  confused with it. Pick another name and say "based on Inkadra" instead.
- Present a hosting service, an app or a company as **the official Inkadra**, or as endorsed by
  or affiliated with the project. Names such as "Inkadra Cloud", "Inkadra Pro" or "Inkadra Hosting"
  are reserved for the project. "Hosting for Inkadra by Example Studio" is fine.
- Publish an app in an app store, a browser extension or a package under the name Inkadra or
  with its logo, other than the ones published by the project.
- Register "Inkadra", or anything confusingly similar, as a trademark, company name, domain
  name or social media account that suggests it is the project.
- Change the logo, or use it as the logo of your own product or service.

If you distribute a modified version, the licences still apply as usual: keep the copyright and
licence notices, and offer your source where the licence requires it. Only the name and the logo
change.

## Asking

If you are not sure, or you would like to use the name or logo in a way this page does not
cover, write to <info@inkadra.com>. Reports of misleading use are welcome at the same address.

"Inkadra" and the Inkadra logo are trademarks of the project's maintainer, used since 2026.
