# Inkadra 0.11.0

Inkadra 0.11 brings its own video player, the same on board cards, in the viewer and for guests of share links. It also replaces the one database lock the whole instance shared with locks per account and per setting, so one person's work no longer waits for another's.

## Highlights

- **A video player of its own:** one dark bar on cards, in the viewer and in the guest viewer, with a timeline that shows what is loaded, the Quality chip on the card too, frame steps while paused, speed, picture in picture and full screen. On a phone the viewer gets large −10 s, play and +10 s buttons and the Quality menu as a bottom sheet. The player keeps its size while the stream climbs to a sharper copy, and copies of portrait videos are named by their short side (480p, not 854p).
- **No more waiting on other people's work:** creating a project, importing an archive, deleting an account or changing a setting used to take one lock for the whole instance. Each now locks only the account, project or setting it touches.
- **Easier to start a project:** an empty board offers Import screenplay, Add Production, Add storyboard and Add moodboard, and New board asks which kind to make. A kind whose module is off shows Turn on instead of disappearing, and when the project already has its Production board, the tile and New board open that one. New project shows the type, shooting dates and team right under the name instead of hiding them under Details.
- **Kinds of to-do lists of your own:** administrators manage the kinds of to-do lists (costumes, props, equipment…) in Settings → Classifications, in a new Production group: add, rename, reorder and archive them. The to-do card menu, the To bring of a scene and of a shooting day, the call sheet and the scene sheets use those names and that order.
- **Told when an open board goes:** when a board is moved to the trash, or it or its project is deleted, while someone has it open, they see it at once, in place of the board, instead of a board that seems to save. Edits not saved yet stay on the device and can be downloaded.

## Read before upgrading

- **Back up first.** This release has database migrations (`0056` to `0058`): the first start of 0.11 writes a backup of the database before migrating, and stops without migrating if it can't (check the free space in `/data/backups`). Going back to 0.10.1 means restoring that backup with 0.10.1.
- **A separate worker restarts with the web.** With `deploy/compose.split.yaml`, `deploy/compose.office.yaml` or `deploy/compose.office.split.yaml`, the worker runs in its own container: update and restart it together with the web (`docker compose up -d` does both). A worker left on 0.10 still takes the old instance lock during imports.
- **A guessable `AUTH_SECRET` stops the start.** In production the server now refuses an `AUTH_SECRET` copied from the documentation or tests, one containing `change-me`, or one with fewer than 8 different characters: the log says `Invalid configuration:` and `AUTH_SECRET: Too easy to guess`. Before upgrading such an instance, move the current value to `AUTH_SECRET_PREVIOUS` in `.env`, set `AUTH_SECRET` to the output of `openssl rand -hex 32`, and restart: everyone signs in again, and two-factor secrets and sealed tokens stay readable ([Upgrading to 0.11](../self-hosting/upgrades.md#upgrading-to-011)).
- **Board documents move from version 27 to 28.** Browser tabs opened before the upgrade stay read-only on boards until they reload, and so does a version of the mobile app that does not know version 28 yet. A board written by 0.11 does not go back to 0.10.1.
- **Stop the old collaboration process before starting the new one.** A collab process now takes a new epoch at start, which the database checks on every board write: an older process still running can no longer overwrite the boards, its writes are refused and it stops. On an orchestrator, update by recreating (stop the old process, then start the new one), not by a rolling update ([Upgrading to 0.11](../self-hosting/upgrades.md#upgrading-to-011)).
- **A start after a crash waits up to 30 seconds.** The web and collab processes no longer take back at once a lease their own container left in Valkey after a crash or an interrupted start.
- **The Clipper's token only clips.** Tokens of the browser extension, those already issued too, are limited to the routes the extension calls once every web and collab process runs 0.11. The extension needs no update, and the Android app's tokens are not affected.
- Nothing else to do with the `compose.yaml` of the release, or with your own copy of an earlier one, apart from that `.env` change where it applies.

## More

- Keyboard in the focused player: Space or K, ← and → 5 s, J and L 10 s, `,` and `.` one frame while paused, M, F and Q.
- At a low board zoom a playing card keeps its buttons large enough to hit; a small card drops controls instead of overflowing.
- The keyboard hint under the viewer is hidden on phones.
- The demo's sample projects show the 0.10 features: a Production board with its Schedule and Troupe, call sheets and set plans.
- Under a shot's characters, the frame panel lists who plays each one, the same way as the call sheet. A character with no performer offers Link, which ties a crew member to the character card (making the card if there is none). In Troupe, a person's chip names the character they play, and the Troupe tour has a new step on it.
- A tutorial started from Settings → Tutorial opens the screen it is about first, such as the storyboard or the Production board, and waits for it. When the project has no board of that kind, it offers to create one; when a view is off or a panel must be opened first, it says so in one line.
- In the start dialog of an empty set plan, Scan the room is now a quiet note after the ready rooms, since it waits for the phone app.
- Menus close with one press of Tab and jump to an item as you type its name; a closed list picks an option as you type, like the browser's own. Screen readers no longer read a person's name twice next to their picture.
- Proofread interface texts in all six languages.
- An invitation works only while the person who made it can still give the access it offers: removing a member, lowering their role or removing their board access also withdraws the pending invitations they can no longer give. Share links made by a deactivated account are suspended, and work again when the account is reactivated.
- Guests of a share link no longer receive who a to-do item is assigned to.
- A card hidden from guests disappears from share links within a second, instead of after the next save (up to about 10 seconds).
- Signing out no longer deletes board copies on the device when it cannot tell whether they hold unsaved edits: it asks first, as for unsaved edits, or keeps them.
- Opening a board with many files asks for their details in a few requests instead of one per file.
- Other people's pointers moving on a board no longer redraw the whole canvas.
- Assigning a to-do item or setting its due date on a large board no longer holds up the server for everyone else while it checks the change.
- Very large SVG files (over 5 MiB) and HEIC files over 256 MiB are kept without thumbnails.

## For administrators

- Migration `0056` gives each member their own order of projects, copied from the shared order at the upgrade; a new project goes after the person's own last one. The web app lists projects in that order and has no way to change it yet.
- A list of projects paged across the upgrade may repeat or skip one project once.
- For native apps, `GET /api/v1/instance/version` lists the capability `per-member-project-order`: `PATCH /projects/:id` with `position` then moves the project only for the caller and needs only the viewer role. An invalid order key answers 400.
- In New project, administrators get a Manage types link on the Type line, which opens Settings → Classifications at Project types.
- **Server page:** a new configuration check warns when `TRUST_PROXY` trusts more than your reverse proxy (`TRUST_PROXY=true`, or a request from a trusted address that did not come through a proxy). Clients could then claim another address, which sign-in limits and the logs rely on. Set `TRUST_PROXY` to the proxy's own address, or to `false` when nothing sits in front of Inkadra ([TRUST_PROXY](../self-hosting/configuration.md#trust_proxy)); with `INKADRA_BIND=0.0.0.0` the default is too wide. The Server page also keeps answering, without charts, when a read from Valkey is slow.
- Migration `0057` limits the Clipper's tokens to the routes it calls ([decision 0137](../decisions/0137-clipper-token-scope.md)). Restoring the backup taken before the upgrade gives them back their earlier reach: revoke again, in Settings → Connected apps, any Clipper token you believe was copied.
- Migration `0058` adds the `collab_epoch` table that fences board writes ([decision 0138](../decisions/0138-collab-fencing-epoch.md)). A collab process that stopped this way leaves what it still held in the `unsaved-boards` folder of its data volume, which the collab process serving that volume merges within a minute. The table is left out of backups: the first start after a restore takes epoch 1.
- The collaboration server refuses updates that no Yjs client writes and that would break a board for everyone, and closes the sender's connection to that board. [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md#what-edit-rights-allow-on-a-board) and [decision 0139](../decisions/0139-collaborative-update-checks.md) say what edit rights allow.
- With Caddy or Nginx in split mode, add `deploy/proxy/<proxy>/compose.split.yaml` after the proxy's `compose.yaml` instead of setting `INKADRA_COLLAB_UPSTREAM`: it also keeps `collab` off the host's ports, as Traefik's already does ([Compose variants](../self-hosting/README.md#compose-variants)).
- The bundled RustFS (`deploy/compose.rustfs.yaml`) runs on the internal network only, without capabilities, read-only, within `RUSTFS_MEM_LIMIT`, `RUSTFS_CPUS` and `RUSTFS_PIDS_LIMIT`.
- **Office previews on Ubuntu:** if the worker log says `bwrap: loopback: Failed RTM_NEWADDR: Operation not permitted`, load Ubuntu's AppArmor profile for bubblewrap with `scripts/bwrap-apparmor.sh` ([Office previews](../self-hosting/office.md#turn-it-on)). The guide's sandbox check now starts bubblewrap from a shell inside the container, as the worker does: started as the container's entrypoint, it failed with that message on Ubuntu 26.04 even where conversion works. CI now converts a Word document in each `-office` image before tagging it.
- Importing a project checks the free space of the data volume before receiving the archive. `/api/v1` answers that set no cache policy of their own now say `Cache-Control: private`, with `no-store`, or `no-cache` when they carry an ETag.
- `GET /projects/:id/cast-appearances` and `GET /projects/:id/plan-kit` read boards one at a time and keep what they derived, instead of blocking other requests; under load right after an upgrade some answer `503` with `Retry-After` until the queue drains.
- For native apps, `GET /api/v1/instance/version` lists new capabilities: `todo-list-catalog` (the kinds of to-do lists in `ClassificationCatalogs.todoLists`), `asset-lookup` (`POST /api/v1/assets/lookup`, the details of up to 200 files in one request), `board-gone-notice` (the collaboration server says why it closed a board) and `diagnostic-proxy-trust`.
- The OpenAPI document now lists every status the server answers, and the server tests check each answer against it; shared components such as `UserSummary` and `Asset` are no longer marked nullable. Regenerate only a client generated from `openapi.json`.

## Fixes

- A board deleted while it was open is no longer written back by the collaboration server, at a save or after a restart (#935).
- Sharing a board with someone, or removing their access, while an account involved was being deleted could fail with a server error, or the deletion of a sole owner could remove the project with the access just given; an editor whose role was lowered meanwhile could still change the access (#945).
- The Resend, Revoke and Copy link buttons of each pending invitation have their own accessible name (#939).
- A portrait video card no longer cuts off its buttons, and the viewer no longer jumps in size while the stream changes quality.
- The short card of a Production board showed a raw translation key in every language.
- A viewer made editor, or an editor made viewer, on an open board waited up to 30 seconds, or a reload, for the change to apply; it now applies at once, as does removing someone's access (#959).
- A person alone on a board saw the header flash Offline about every 30 seconds (#965).
- After leaving a board, the Storyboard and Shot list counts in the sidebar fell back to old numbers, or 0 for a new board, for up to a minute (#966).
- On Home, the cover of a Production board with shooting days read "Empty board"; it now shows the number of shooting days (#955).
- The Schedule's queue counted a waiting shot twice, once with its scene; it now reads, for example, "1 shot · 1 scene" (#956).
- Delete, Backspace or ⌘Z pressed in an open menu, such as Arrange or New block, no longer acts on the board or the Schedule behind it (#953).
- An open board kept saying it was synced for up to 30 seconds after it or its project was deleted, and dropped the edits made meanwhile (#951).
- On a set plan setup, an icon for the base's Set was refused when the setup was full; a lighting kit with one slot left was placed without its modifiers, and a layer chosen for an icon could be ignored.
- In the search palette, an action could move under the pointer while a search answered, so the click missed it.
- The Server page failed with a server error when a read from Valkey timed out.
