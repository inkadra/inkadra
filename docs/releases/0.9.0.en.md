# Inkadra 0.9.0

## Highlights

- Inkadra on a phone, in the browser: home with Unsorted, the card sheet, the full-screen note, the call sheet, client review and swipes in the Inbox.
- Attendance: organisers link the crew to accounts and see who confirmed; linked people confirm from their phone.
- Report a problem from inside the app, with the screenshots, videos and logs you choose and a preview before sending.
- Spanish, German, French and Brazilian Portuguese, with English as the fallback language.
- A new contextual card toolbar, redesigned frames and drawing, and videos that play inside their card.

## Read before upgrading

- **Reports to the developers:** every instance where no administrator saved a choice becomes able to send reports to the Inkadra developers, even if they were off until now. No report leaves unless someone writes it, sees its preview and sends it. Selected text, screenshots, videos, files and logs leave the server and pass through Cloudflare, which sees the server's IP address. To avoid this, set an empty `FEEDBACK_RELAY_URL=` before upgrading, then save a choice in Settings → About → Reports. Details in `docs/self-hosting/configuration.md`.
- **Email language:** people who never opened the language setting get emails in English until they come back to the app. This includes Italian accounts that got Italian emails until now.
- **Templates removed:** board and project templates are gone. Existing projects and boards stay as they are.
- **Open tabs:** board documents move to a newer version. Tabs and installed apps open from before the upgrade stay read-only until they reload.

## More

- Richer character sheets, and shot size, angle and movement catalogs in Classifications.
- Comments with explicit audiences, and a set plan editor with two bars.
- Exports in social formats, 16:9 pages, approved frames only, a kickoff summary and the call sheet.
- Every email redone in one shared layout.

## For administrators

- The upgrade applies database migrations at startup, as usual.
- The official [Inkadra Clipper](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/inkadra-clipper/hdajoikapihpjfbgioeagenmoalckjkh) is now on the Chrome Web Store, and this server is ready for it. On older servers it can't sign in until you update, or until an administrator adds its id, `hdajoikapihpjfbgioeagenmoalckjkh`, to `CLIPPER_EXTENSION_IDS`.
- Office conversion has its own queue: an `all` container no longer takes jobs meant for the `-office` worker.
- The four new languages are translated with AI and not yet reviewed by native speakers.
- Exporting and importing a project loses crew links and attendance answers.

## Known limits

- The phone screens were tried by hand on Android with Chrome. Not on an iPhone or iPad with Safari, nor with VoiceOver or TalkBack.
- An open call sheet follows changes made elsewhere within about a minute, not instantly.

## Fixes

- The note bar and dialog buttons no longer end up under the keyboard on Android.
- Inbox dates follow calendar days.
- Fixes from testing 0.8 across the board, media, screenplays and settings.
