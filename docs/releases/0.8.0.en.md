# Inkadra 0.8.0

## Highlights

- Set plans: an icon library, scenes, lenses, blocking, layers and variants of the same plan.
- Screenplays: Inkadra recognises screenplays dropped on the board and also imports PDF scripts.
- A private calendar feed with shooting days and deadlines, to add to your phone's calendar.
- Export presets for whoever receives them (client pitch, production book, internal kickoff), with the project theme and readable PNGs, one image per page.
- Projects grouped by client or series, and the needed items rolled up by shooting day.

## More

- Personal preferences, and a board that stays around its content.
- Colour swatches with HEX and RGB, copy the value, and a palette taken from an image.
- Optional email news when the instance is updated, opt in from Notifications.
- Review is available in projects without a storyboard when there is a client review link.
- Table preview of CSV and XLSX files, with "Turn into table" for the first sheet.
- 3D model details and specimens of uploaded fonts.

## For administrators

- The upgrade applies database migrations on start, as usual.
- Office document previews (DOCX, PPTX and similar) are optional and off by default.
  They need the worker on the `-office` image and are still experimental: see `docs/self-hosting/office.md`.

## Fixes

- Importing exported projects with collaborative text, RAW thumbnails, the plan toolbar on phones.
- Member management on phones, sessions after a password change, dates inside exported ZIP files.
- Plan scale label, PDF screenplay clean-up and voice-over character names.
